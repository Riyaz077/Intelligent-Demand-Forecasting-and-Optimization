# Model Card

- **Task:** daily product/store demand forecasting.
- **Models:** Random Forest and HistGradientBoosting regression; seasonal-naive lag-7 baseline.
- **Inputs:** product/store identifiers, category, price, promotions, simulated temperature, holiday and lag/rolling demand features.
- **Training data:** synthetic dataset generated by `src/data.py`.
- **Evaluation:** chronological holdout with MAE, RMSE and R².
- **Limitations:** no live ERP/POS integration, probabilistic intervals, stockout censoring, supply constraints, product substitution or validated real-world exogenous signals.
- **Appropriate use:** education, demonstration and controlled experimentation only.
